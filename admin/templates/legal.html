Privacy Policy

{{ channel }} publishing tool · {{ domain }}

Summary

This private tool publishes the channel owner's own videos to the owner's own accounts. It does not collect, sell or share personal data of viewers or of any other person.

Data the tool handles

  • Account authorisation tokens granted by the channel owner for YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. They are stored on the owner's private server and used only to publish videos and read the owner's own channel statistics.
  • Basic profile and statistics of the owner's accounts (display name, follower, like and view counts), read through the platforms' APIs and shown only to the owner in the administration pages.
  • The videos, titles and descriptions that the owner publishes.

Data the tool does not handle

The tool does not read viewers' profiles, comments' authors, messages or contact lists, and it does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. The only cookie is a sign-in session cookie for the owner.

Sharing

Data is sent only to the platforms the owner has connected (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok), in order to publish the owner's videos. Nothing is shared with any other third party.

Retention and deletion

Authorisation tokens are kept until the owner disconnects an account or revokes access in the platform's settings, which makes them unusable immediately. Working files of videos are deleted from the server after 30 days.
